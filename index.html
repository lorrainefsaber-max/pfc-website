Polytechnic Fishing Corporation

Polytechnic Fishing Corporation, or PFC, is a fishing and fish distribution company that handles fish from fishing operations through processing, packaging, inventory management, and distribution.

The company focuses on maintaining proper handling procedures to help preserve the quality of fish products before they reach retailers and customers.
