About Us

Home > About Us

Polytechnic Fishing Corporation

Polytechnic Fishing Corporation (PFC) is a fishing distribution company that provides different fish products to meet the needs of consumers and businesses.

Our operations begin with the sourcing of fish from the sea and continue through proper handling, processing, packaging, and distribution. PFC focuses on maintaining product quality and freshness while ensuring that each product reaches customers safely and efficiently.

Our goal is to provide accessible and quality products while building a reliable connection between fishermen, markets, retailers, and consumers.

Mission

To provide fresh, quality, and accessible fish products through responsible sourcing, proper handling, and efficient distribution while maintaining customer satisfaction and supporting the fishing industry.

Vision

To become a trusted company known for quality products, reliable distribution, and responsible practices in the fishing industry.
