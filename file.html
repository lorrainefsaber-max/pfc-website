About PFC

Polytechnic Fishing Corporation (PFC) is a fishing and fish distribution company focused on providing quality fish products.

Our operations cover fishing, proper handling, processing, packaging, inventory management, and distribution of fish products.

We aim to provide reliable products while maintaining responsible practices throughout our operations.
